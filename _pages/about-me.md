Western classical music is a longstanding interest, particularly the works of [Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfgang_Amadeus_Mozart) and [Johann Sebastian Bach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Sebastian_Bach). I often return to Mozart's [_Requiem_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Requiem_%28Mozart%29) and [_Symphony No. 40_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symphony_No._40_%28Mozart%29), and Bach's [_Cello Suites_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cello_Suites_%28Bach%29), [_Air on the G String_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_on_the_G_String), and [_Toccata and Fugue in D minor_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toccata_and_Fugue_in_D_minor,_BWV_565). Other favourites include [Johann Pachelbel's _Canon in D_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pachelbel%27s_Canon) and the storm movement from [Antonio Vivaldi's _The Four Seasons_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Four_Seasons_%28Vivaldi%29).

I also listen to [The Beatles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Beatles), [folk music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folk_music), and [country music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_music), with a particular interest in lyrical storytelling.

I admire [Richard Feynman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Feynman) for his curiosity, clarity of thought, and approach to scientific problems. [Bob Dylan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_Dylan)'s songwriting and [Rabindranath Tagore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabindranath_Tagore)'s poetry, music, and humanism are also lasting influences. [Rabindra Sangeet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabindra_Sangeet) holds a central place in my cultural life.

My interests in cinema include the films of [Satyajit Ray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satyajit_Ray) and [Quentin Tarantino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino). I appreciate Ray's close observation of people and society, and Tarantino's dialogue and visual style. I also write poetry in **Bengali and English**.

I play chess and enjoy studying the games of [Mikhail Tal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Tal), particularly his imaginative attacking play. My interest in astronomy led to involvement with the **NISER Astronomy Club**, where I helped organize talks and observing sessions. At NISER, I also participated in **Vikiran**, the institute's physics club.
