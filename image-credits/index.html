d-wave gap on a square-lattice Fermi surface Schematic

Schematic model visualization. Fermi contour of ε(k) = −2t(cos kx + cos ky) − 4t′ cos kx cos ky − μ with t′/t = −0.3 at filling n = 0.85, coloured by the dx²−y² form factor Δ(k) = Δ₀(cos kx − cos ky). Dashed lines mark the nodal diagonals.

Spin-split Fermi surfaces of a d-wave altermagnet Schematic

Schematic model visualization. Simplified dx²−y²-type altermagnet, E↑/↓(k) = −2t(cos kx + cos ky) − μ ± λ(cos kx − cos ky) with λ/t = 0.6, μ/t = −2.4. The spin splitting vanishes on the zone diagonals.

Chiral edge modes of a Chern-insulator ribbon Schematic

Schematic model visualization. Spectrum of the Qi–Wu–Zhang Chern-insulator model (m = 1, Chern number 1) on a ribbon 40 sites wide. Colour marks localisation on the upper (blue) or lower (teal) edge; each edge hosts one chiral mode crossing the bulk gap.
