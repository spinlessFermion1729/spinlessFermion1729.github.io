    Exotic Electronic Order in a Parabolic Kagome Semimetal
    C. Alexander Baum, Jonas Issing, Sarbajit Mazumdar, and 4 more authors
    arXiv:2607.18389 (2026) Preprint
    Abs DOI arXiv Bib

    We study an interacting kagome-lattice realization of a quadratic band-touching semimetal at 2/3 filling with onsite and nearest-neighbor repulsive interactions. Combining functional renormalization group and slave-boson approaches, we map its phase diagram from intermediate to strong coupling and uncover a hierarchy of unconventional electronic orders.
