About

I am a Ph.D. student in theoretical condensed matter physics in the group of Prof. Ronny Thomale at the Chair of Theoretical Physics I, Julius-Maximilians-Universität Würzburg.

My research asks how electronic correlations, lattice symmetry, and topology organize electrons into collective quantum phases. Current topics include unconventional superconductivity at strong coupling, altermagnetic order and its collective spin excitations, and quantum transport in flat-band and topological systems.

Methodologically, I combine slave-boson and fluctuation-based many-body approaches with tight-binding modelling and scattering theory. The aim is a controlled link between microscopic mechanisms and experimentally accessible signatures in quantum materials.

    Exotic Electronic Order in a Parabolic Kagome Semimetal
    C. Alexander Baum, Jonas Issing, Sarbajit Mazumdar, and 4 more authors
    arXiv:2607.18389 (2026)
    Abs DOI arXiv Bib

    We study an interacting kagome-lattice realization of a quadratic band-touching semimetal at 2/3 filling with onsite and nearest-neighbor repulsive interactions. Combining functional renormalization group and slave-boson approaches, we map its phase diagram from intermediate to strong coupling and uncover a hierarchy of unconventional electronic orders.
