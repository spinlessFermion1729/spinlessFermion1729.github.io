Research

Superconducting pairing, altermagnetic order, collective spin excitations, and quantum transport in correlated systems.

My research examines how electronic correlations, lattice symmetry, and band topology shape electronic order, collective excitations, and transport. A central part of this work uses slave-boson approaches to Hubbard models to treat strong local correlations and calculate momentum- and frequency-resolved response functions.

01 · Research direction

Unconventional Superconductivity

Superconducting pairing mediated by electronic fluctuations in the presence of strong local correlations.

Models
One-band Hubbard model on the square lattice; kagome lattice at 2/3 filling with onsite and nearest-neighbour repulsion
Methods
Spin-rotation-invariant Kotliar–Ruckenstein slave bosons, fluctuation-derived pairing vertex, frequency-dependent gap equation; functional renormalization group (kagome study)

To investigate pairing beyond weak-coupling approaches, we developed a spin-rotation-invariant Kotliar–Ruckenstein slave-boson framework in which an effective pairing vertex is obtained from fluctuations around the mean-field saddle point. Solving the resulting frequency-dependent gap equation for the square-lattice Hubbard model allows us to examine pairing instabilities as a function of doping, interaction strength, and temperature, and to resolve the gap structure on the real-frequency axis.

  • S. Mazumdar et al., Phys. Rev. B 114, 194511 (2026) · arXiv:2605.26233
  • C. A. Baum et al., arXiv:2607.18389, electronic order in a kagome quadratic band-touching semimetal from intermediate to strong coupling

02 · Research direction

Altermagnetism and Collective Spin Excitations

Microscopic selection of altermagnetic order and the evolution of collective spin excitations across the metal–insulator transition.

Models
Hubbard models on the square-kagome and checkerboard lattices
Methods
Kotliar–Ruckenstein slave-boson theory, sublattice-resolved magnetic instabilities, dynamical spin susceptibilities, magnon dispersions and lifetimes

Altermagnets combine compensated magnetic order with momentum-dependent spin splitting constrained by crystal symmetry. I investigate the microscopic selection of these phases and the influence of electronic correlations on their collective spin excitations. On the square-kagome lattice, we examine sublattice-resolved magnetic instabilities associated with $d_{xy}$- and $d_{x^2-y^2}$-type altermagnetic phases. On the checkerboard lattice, we track chiral magnon branches across the metal–insulator transition, from strongly damped modes in the metal to coherent branches with modified dispersion in the Mott insulator.

  • J. Issing et al., Phys. Rev. B 114, 115128 (2026) · arXiv:2603.12022
  • J. Issing et al., arXiv:2605.11669, altermagnons at the metal–insulator transition

03 · Research direction

Quantum Transport, Band Geometry, and Topology

Signatures of flat bands, band geometry, and topology in superconducting transport, nonlinear Hall response, and charge pumping.

Models
Proximitized junctions on the extended α-T3 lattice; C3-symmetric metal–organic frameworks downfolded to star- and honeycomb-lattice models; non-Hermitian Rice–Mele model
Methods
Scattering theory, wave-packet dynamics, Josephson-junction transport, Berry-curvature and symmetry analysis, non-Bloch topological invariants

I investigate how band structure and geometry influence transport in several complementary settings. In proximity-coupled junctions on the extended $\alpha$-$T_3$ lattice, we study enhanced Andreev reflection associated with quasi-flat bands, direction-dependent Goos–Hänchen-like shifts, and a Hall-like response in Josephson junctions. In metal–organic frameworks, we examine how spin–orbit coupling and broken inversion symmetry generate Berry curvature near gapped Dirac points and enable a nonlinear Hall response. Earlier work on the non-Hermitian Rice–Mele model investigated the roles of the skin effect and exceptional points in anomalous charge pumping, including regimes with no Hermitian counterpart.

  • S. Mazumdar et al., Phys. Rev. B 113, 134517 (2026) · arXiv:2507.06327
  • S. Mazumdar et al., arXiv:2603.09612, nonlinear Hall effect in metal–organic frameworks
  • A. Kumar et al., J. Phys.: Condens. Matter 37, 135601 (2025) · arXiv:2110.06797
